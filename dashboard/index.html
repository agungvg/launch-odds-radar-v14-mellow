Is the printed range honest?

Ranges come from the leave-one-out residual spread (0.86 dex): P25–P75 is the model ×10±0.58, P10–P90 is ×10±1.10. Wide, because the honest spread is wide — the drawer shows the same width broken down per protocol.

Already trading — the market’s own values, never mixed into the board

Model radar-14: ridge on log10 TVL plus shrunk category effects (ridge 16); odds: logistic on age, family and size. Farm rule: the modelled net must beat a 4% T-bill on the same deposit and $25 an hour, over the horizon, at the drop share. Yield earned meanwhile is left out. A farm plan costs $15.00 of gas over 12 weeks and 3.3 hours of hands-on time.

Method note: the walk-forward split trains on the 66 oldest listed tokens and tests on the 29 newest; the 47/48 split is the robustness check. No category-rate leakage anywhere: the model never uses a category’s mean ratio. All figures on this page are recomputed in your browser from the same data file the table reads.
